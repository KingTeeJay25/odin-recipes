Mac 'n Cheese Recipe

This is a recipe to simple creamy Mac 'n Cheese that will have you singing in your sleep!

Ingredients

  • 225g of Macaroni
  • 1 cup whole milk (or heavy cream for extra richness)
  • 1.5 to 2 cups of freshly shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp cream cheese
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp paprika or black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta: Cook the macaroni in salted boiling wated in accordance to the direction on the packaging
  2. Melt the Base: Return the hot pot to low heat. Melt the butter and stir in the cream cheese and milk until combined and warm
  3. Melt the cheese: Turn off the heat completely (prevents the cheese from getting grainy). Add the shredded chess in two batches stiring continously until melted into a smooth sauce
  4. Combine & Season: Fold the cooked macaroni back into the pot with garlic powder, paprika and a pinch of salt. Stir for 1 minute until every noodle is coated in sauce.
