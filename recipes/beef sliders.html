Beef Slidders Recipe

This is a simple recipe of delicious beef slidders that will have you tap dancing!

Ingredients

  • 450g of ground beef
  • 12-packs slidder rolls
  • 6 slices of Cheddar, Gouda or American cheese
  • 1/2 tsp of garlic power
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 of salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Prep the Rolls: Preheat your oven to 190 C. Slice the entire 12-pack of rolls horizontally in half so you have one large top and bottom piece
  2. Form & Season the Beef: Press the ground beef into a single larger rectangular patty roughly the same size as the connected rolls. Season the top generously with the salt, pepper, garlic power and onion powerder
  3. Cook the beef
  4. Assemble
  5. Butter and Bake
