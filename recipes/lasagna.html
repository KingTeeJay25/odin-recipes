Lasagna Recipes

This is a recipe to a simple and very flavourful lasagna. This recipe if quick and easy, meaning if you are lazy like me you are in the write place.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 32-45 oz jar marinara or pasta sauce
  • 9-12 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
  • 1 large egg (mixed with cheese)
  • 2-4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook the meat: Brown the ground beef and chopped onion in a pan over medium heat, then drain extra fat. Stir in most of the marinara sauce and simmer.
  2. Boil the noodles: Cook the lasagna noodles in boiling salted water until al dente, then drain.
  3. Mix the cheese: Stir the ricotta cheese and egg together in a bowl.
  4. Layer the pan: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a greased 9x13-inch baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, a layer of the ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat the layers.
  5. Top and bake: Finish with a final layer of noodles, remaining sauce, and a generous topping of mozzarella and Parmesan. Cover with foil and bake at 375°F for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake for 15 more minutes until bubbly.
  6. Rest and serve: Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing
